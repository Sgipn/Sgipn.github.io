AI Agent · Text-to-SQL

Text-to-SQL Agent for Revenue and Churn Analytics

Converts plain-English business questions into validated SQL, letting stakeholders self-serve revenue and churn metrics without waiting on an analyst. Two independent safety layers keep every generated query read-only and schema-approved.

  • Enforced two-layer safety: AST parsing via sqlglot restricts queries to SELECT-only on approved views, backed by read-only DuckDB execution as a failsafe.
